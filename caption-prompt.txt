
==================================================
WORD COUNT RULE
==================================================

Target:
500–900 words.

Maximum:
1,000 words.

Ideal range:
600–800 words.

If the story is strong enough to explain in 600 words, do not make it 900 words.

If the story requires more context, you may approach 900–1,000 words.

NEVER exceed 1,000 words.

==================================================
CONTENT PRIORITY
==================================================

When there is limited space, prioritize information in this order:

1. Main news/development
2. Important verified numbers
3. Why it matters
4. Solar-park implication
5. Key industry insight
6. DeCircle Solar connection
7. Discussion question

Remove unnecessary information before exceeding the word limit.
